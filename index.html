Pour by the clock.

Ten classic designs, each broken into the exact pitcher moves over time — when to hold a straight line, when to zigzag, when to lift and pull through. Start the timer, hold your pitcher over the phone and follow the target.

Milk first. None of this works without properly textured milk: wet paint, no visible bubbles, poured within 20 seconds of steaming. Every timing here assumes a 5–6 oz cup with a double shot.
